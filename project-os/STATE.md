### ServiceOS — the rota (new module)
- [x] **SRV-01 — naming what needs doing, and counting who did it** (`7d32baa`, `1f256ca`). Charley's spec, answered in four questions before building. **A duty is a rule, not a table of dates**: "Trash, Tuesdays, 30 minutes" is one row and its Tuesdays are computed on the way to the screen, the way room slots are — no horizon to top up, nothing stale when an organiser moves the day. `duty_claims` holds only turns somebody took. Claiming is per occurrence (Charley: "pick specific dates to claim"), or **standing** — adopt the bins and you own the Tuesdays nobody has computed yet, materialised ahead so the duty still shows a name against it rather than vanishing into an arrangement that appears nowhere. Hours: the duty's estimate is credited and the member may correct it with a note, recorded as a correction. Three surfaces — **Serve** (portal, by day), **My Service** (member), **Serving** (organiser) — each in the nav section its audience already looks in, with a reachability test. Refusals worth keeping: a date the duty does not fall on, a date already past, a turn already covered, banking somebody else's turn, and **deleting a duty that carries hours** (retired instead — the cascade would erase the record members are measured against). Two pure modules carry the arithmetic and are tested alone: occurrences (DST-correct) and expectation (windows, proration).
- [x] **Tier service expectations** — minutes over a week, month or year, or none, which is the default and true of every tier that exists today. **Calendar windows, not anniversaries**, so "who is short" is a list an organiser can read across the co-op; **first window prorated from the join date**, or everybody who joins in December arrives already in arrears. Weeks run Sunday–Saturday, following the `0 = Sunday` numbering the room hours already use.
- [x] **SRV-02 — service hours as contributed value in ImpactOS** (`a8f3d6d`). Hours reach the impact report, the Measuring page and `GET /orgs/:orgId/service/contribution`. **No dollar figure until the co-op sets a rate** (`Organization.volunteerHourValueCents`, nullable, no default): hours need no assumption to be true, and a rate MaybeOS chose would be asserted in the co-op's name in a document a funder reads. When a rate exists the block says it is the co-op's own and that MaybeOS does not supply one. Counts only turns marked done, on the day the turn fell (not the day it was recorded), a count of people and never a list, and reports how many turns the member re-timed — the honest measure of how much of the total is estimate.
- [x] **SRV-03 — what a host does before, during and after a booking** (`ede5553`). An admin writes three lists and the messages that carry them; MaybeOS emails the host on the day. Charley's defaults: Before and During at 07:00 on the day of the booking, After an hour before it ends. **Not the SRV-01 `duties` table** — nobody claims these and nobody volunteers for them; they come with the room, they are always the host's, and they recur because bookings do. The two defaults are two different *kinds* of schedule, which is what the `anchor` field is for: "7am on the day" is a clock time in the co-op's timezone, "an hour before it ends" is an offset from an instant, and expressing the first as minutes-before-start would give a different time of day for every booking. Phases due at the same moment arrive as one email, so the defaults produce two rather than three. **Off by absence, not by a flag**: a co-op has no briefings until an admin writes one, and there is no enable switch because "no message" and "off" are the same state. Three guards: a **two-hour grace window** (every past booking is "due and never sent", so without a floor switching this on emails everybody who has ever booked a room), **booked-too-late** (a member booking at 09:00 for that afternoon is not sent the 7am message), and **the notice is written before the email** (no jobs table by design, so that row is what stands between a retried invocation and four copies). Verified by booting the DI container and running the real `runDueTasks` against dev, then reading the email it composed for a genuine booking.
- [x] **`EmailService.sendRaw` now reports whether it sent.** It swallowed provider failures and returned void, so SRV-03's scheduler reported "sent 1" for an email Postmark had refused — and because the notice is written first, that was a briefing nobody received and nothing retried. It still never throws: every caller sends email as a side effect of something more important, and a failed notification must not roll back the thing it was notifying about. On a rejection SRV-03 takes its notice back and retries, bounded by the grace window rather than a counter. The two Belonging callers ignore the return and are unaffected.

- **IMP-15 — the ticket-purchase touchpoint has no ticketing.** It is one of the PRD's four P0 touchpoints, and `Event` has no price field: the only thing MaybeOS charges for is membership dues, and `Room.hourlyRate` is stored and never charged (SPC-06). Building event ticketing is a product surface — pricing, checkout, refunds, guest purchase — not a gap to fill in passing. **It also blocks two other things**: "revenue by activity" can currently produce dues and nothing else, and the PRD's "spend tier" segment has no source at all.
- **IMP-16 — the financial section has no expense side.** No expense, budget or invoice model exists; MaybeOS knows what came in and nothing about what went out. **The roadmap said this "should gate §7"; reading the PRD, it does not gate v1.** P0 asks only for "basic financial derivation: revenue by activity, in-kind/subsidy value" — both computable today, since pay-what-you-can already exists on tiers. Cost-per-outcome and mission-alignment-of-spend are **P1**. This is also the PRD's own open question 7, which asks whether expenses live outside the platform at all and whether the composites should be gated behind a completeness check. Worth answering before building an accounting model into a product whose non-goals say "not a replacement for accounting".

## How production ships
- **`next build` and `next dev` share `.next`, so running the production build kills a running dev server.** It leaves the dev process serving production artefacts and every page throws `Cannot find module './vendor-chunks/…'`. Stop the preview, build, then restart it — or expect to `rm -rf apps/web/.next` and restart anyway. Hit twice on 2026-09-03 and diagnosed as a code bug both times.
- **Before pushing web changes, run `npm --workspace @maybeos/web run verify` and read its exit code.** Netlify runs `next build`, which treats **ESLint errors as fatal** — so a change that typechecks and passes tests can still fail the deploy. On 2026-09-03 four consecutive production builds failed on five unused imports, and the local check reported "LINT CLEAN" because it grepped the lint output for a pattern that matched nothing and then echoed a hardcoded success. **Never grep a tool's output to decide whether it passed; use its exit code.** For a definitive answer, run the deploy's own command: `npm --workspace @maybeos/web run build`.
- **Netlify builds `claude/maybeOS-suite-foundation-1Wauk`** — origin's default branch, confirmed by Charley 2026-08-11. There is **no `main` branch**, and no staging step: **merging into that branch is the production deploy of maybeos.org.** Build and test on the merged result *before* pushing, not after. **DEPLOY.md now documents the Netlify deploy** — it was a Railway walkthrough with a correction notice on top, which is a document that eventually gets followed.
- **Charley has delegated releases** (2026-08-11): merge and push without asking, report what went live afterwards. Prefer `--no-ff` so a release stays revertable as one commit. Still ask first for: Stripe live mode, destructive operations against the production database, secret rotation, or anything needing a credential in plaintext.
- **Caveat:** the Claude Code auto-mode classifier blocks `git merge` and blocks editing the permission settings that would allow it. That gate is the harness's, not Charley's. Charley added `Bash(git merge:*)` to `.claude/settings.json` on 2026-08-11, which lifts it. That file is **deliberately not committed** — it grants an agent merge rights and OSS-01 has this repo heading for open source, so committing it is Charley's call.
- **Scheduled work runs every 15 minutes** via the `scheduled-tasks` Netlify function (D-022). It is not reachable over HTTP: Netlify returns 403 to external callers (verified — a function that does not exist returns 404), and the handler itself refuses any invocation without a `next_run` payload. **Not yet observed firing in production**: production has no proposals or surveys, so a run leaves no trace, and confirming it needs the Netlify function logs — i.e. the token in OPS-12. Sentry cron check-ins would make this self-reporting without a schema change.
